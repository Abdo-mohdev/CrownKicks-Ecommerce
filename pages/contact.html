Quick Answers

The most common questions we get. Chances are your answer is already here.

How long does shipping take?
Standard delivery within Cairo takes 1–2 business days. Other cities in Egypt take 3–5 days. All orders over $100 ship free.
Are all products 100% authentic?
Absolutely. Every single pair we carry is sourced directly from authorized distributors and brand partners. We stake our reputation on authenticity — no exceptions.
Can I return or exchange my order?
Yes — you have 14 days from delivery to request a return or exchange, as long as the shoes are unworn and in original packaging. Limited edition drops are final sale.
How do I get early access to limited drops?
Follow us on Instagram and TikTok — that's where we announce raffles and early access first. You can also sign up to our newsletter for priority notifications.
Do you ship outside Egypt?
We currently ship across Egypt and are working on expanding to the wider MENA region. Stay tuned — regional shipping is coming soon.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash on delivery, credit & debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), Vodafone Cash, and InstaPay. All online payments are fully secured.
