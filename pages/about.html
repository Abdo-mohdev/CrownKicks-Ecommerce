Who We Are

More Than A Store

Premium Shoes EG started as an idea between two sneaker-obsessed friends frustrated with the lack of authentic, curated footwear in the market. We wanted a place where the culture came first — where every drop meant something.

Today we're proud to be one of Egypt's most recognized sneaker destinations, stocking everything from iconic heritage silhouettes to the most heat-seeking limited editions. Our mission hasn't changed — put the right pair on the right person.

We don't just sell shoes. We tell stories through soles. Every collab, every exclusive, every restock is carefully chosen to reflect who you are and the statement you want to make.

Karim & Youssef
Co-Founders, Premium Shoes EG
Est. 2018 Cairo, Egypt

Our Journey

2018
The Beginning

Two friends, one garage, and a shared obsession with sneakers. Founded with a seed inventory of 40 pairs.

2019
First Retail Location

Opened our flagship store in Maadi, Cairo. Sold out in 3 days and knew we were onto something real.

2021
Going Online

Launched our e-commerce platform. Egypt-wide orders within weeks of launch.

2023
Rebranded & Grew

Rebranded as Premium Shoes EG. Hit 50,000 pairs sold. The community made us who we are.

2025+
What's Next

Expanding across MENA, launching our own exclusive label, building the biggest sneaker community in the Arab world.
